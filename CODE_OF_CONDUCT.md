# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing changes. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks. Respect others' time and privacy, and help newcomers understand the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, or other community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. If you experience or witness a conduct problem, use the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ryota-murakami) to arrange a private conversation. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

This policy applies to everyone participating in the project, including maintainers.
